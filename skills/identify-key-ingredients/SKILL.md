# Identify key ingredients

Adapted from Pharos; see https://github.com/YijunYuan/math-paper-rewrite/blob/main/NOTICE.md and references/upstream.md.

## Inputs
Use the key-step map from ../identify-key-steps/SKILL.md or locate those steps in the supplied proof. Read relevant source passages, references, and any author-provided guidance. Missing research logs are not required inputs.

## Trace and test
For each nontrivial step, identify the input that makes it work:
- An observation, invariant, reformulation, auxiliary construction, or parameter choice developed in the argument.
- An external result: precise authors/title, theorem or page, version if known, and the hypotheses required at its use.
- An imported method: its source, the adaptation made here, and the conclusion that adaptation enables.
- A supplied human contribution: attribute only what the supplied record supports.

Ask what fails if the input is removed and whether a routine substitute is available. Rank by logical importance, not citation count or prose length. Do not infer importance from institutional prestige.

Where a contribution depends on a source, compare the cited statement with its actual use. Use an accessible primary source when available; otherwise mark the attribution or applicability unresolved. A source not located is not thereby proved nonexistent. Never invent theorem numbers, quotations, references, or human contributions.

Distinguish prior work from an adaptation and from a potentially new claim. Say “not found in the sources examined” rather than claiming first-ever novelty without a sufficiently broad literature basis. Do not start a comprehensive literature review unless requested or necessary for a specific claim.

## Output
For each ingredient give: category, concise description, source anchor, entry point in the proof, what it enables, what breaks without it, and unresolved questions.
Then provide the factual ingredients for a contribution paragraph. If asked to write that paragraph, describe mathematical mechanisms and limitations; keep agents, routes, logs, and internal record identifiers out of it. Record AI-use information separately when the author requests disclosure.
