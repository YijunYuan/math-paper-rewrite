# Paper style

Adapted from Pharos; see https://github.com/YijunYuan/math-paper-rewrite/blob/main/NOTICE.md and references/upstream.md. This skill controls expression, not mathematical truth.

## Resolve the style
Use the author's explicit instructions and journal requirements first. If the supplied project has style/STYLE.md, read it and any accessible exemplar it names. Otherwise use references/STYLE.md bundled here.
Do not open unrelated private files to search for exemplars. If a requested exemplar is unavailable, say so and use the available style rules.

Treat an exemplar as evidence about sentence structure, exposition, and density. Do not copy its claims, distinctive passages, bibliography, or author information into the new manuscript.

## Apply
Keep a consistent voice, terminology, notation, level of detail, and paragraph structure across sections. Use clear statements of what a paragraph proves and why the next argument is needed.
Explain mathematical mechanisms rather than repeatedly claiming importance, novelty, elegance, or broad applicability.
Preserve technical qualifications and necessary repetition. Remove an empty transition rather than replacing it with another stock phrase.
Keep the manuscript's language unless translation was requested. A Chinese-language task does not imply translating an English paper.

Use the default introduction/section patterns as adaptable guidance, not a requirement to add sections to a short paper or overwrite a journal template.
Preserve theorem hypotheses, conclusions, labels, citation support, and proof dependencies. Surface suspected mathematical errors separately.
Never infer AI authorship from stylistic patterns or claim to make writing evade detectors. Improve clarity and specificity.

## Output
When resolving style for a larger task, give a compact style sheet and apply it to the requested scope. When editing, return revised text and material changes; do not force a separate style report unless useful.
Keep AI disclosure, funding, author contributions, and acknowledgements grounded in supplied information. A stylistic preference is not authorization to invent or remove them.
